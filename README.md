Superstore Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard

Project Overview

This project analyzes Superstore sales and profit data using Microsoft Excel to identify business trends, evaluate regional performance, and understand customer segments and product profitability.

Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Excel
- PivotTables
- PivotCharts
- Data Analysis & Visualization

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Total Sales: ₹22,97,200.86
- Total Profit: ₹2,86,397.02
- Total Quantity: 37,873
- Total Orders: 9,994
- Average Order Value: ₹229.86

Key Business Insights

- Technology generates the highest profit among the three product categories.
- The Consumer segment contributes the highest sales.
- The West region records the highest sales among the regions.
- Standard Class contributes the highest sales among the shipping modes.
- Sales fluctuate over time, with higher sales toward the later period.

Dashboard Features

- Sales and profit analysis by category
- Regional sales and profit comparison
- Sales analysis by customer segment
- Monthly sales trends from 2014 to 2017
- Top 10 products by sales
- Shipping mode performance analysis
